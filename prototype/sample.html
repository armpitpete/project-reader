Why should this assessment be trusted?

The completion score uses recognised owner-authority records pinned to the source commit. Open issues, pull requests and activity are shown as evidence, but they do not silently define completion. When the evidence is missing or contradictory, Project Reader must say unknown.

A completed project is marked as already complete rather than given a speculative future probability.
